What the apps collect

Our apps read hardware information (drive health, temperatures, battery data, system configuration) and process it entirely on your Mac. None of this data is sent to us.

License activation

When you activate a license, the app contacts our licensing provider to verify your key. This sends the license key and an anonymous device identifier. No hardware readings are included.

Purchases

Payments are handled by our reseller / merchant of record. They collect your name, email, billing address and payment details to process the order. We receive your name, email and order details so we can deliver your license and provide support. We never see your full card number.
