Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

This website

This site has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the service running, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Email

If you email us — for example to be notified when an app launches — we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to send the launch news you asked for. We don't sell or share it, and we'll delete it if you ask.

Our apps

Our apps read diagnostic information on your Mac — Wi-Fi link details (network name, access point, signal, channel), network configuration, connection test results and system log lines — and process it on that Mac. The apps have no accounts and send no data to us. Evidence bundles are saved locally and leave the Mac only when someone exports them.

Changes

If this changes — for example when apps go on sale and a payment provider is involved — we'll update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

Questions or deletion requests: support@anothernetworkingguy.com.
