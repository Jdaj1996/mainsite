Why not the App Store?

Real diagnostics need low-level access to sensors and drive data that sandboxed App Store apps aren't allowed. Selling directly lets these apps actually do their job.

Still safe to install

Every build is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS verifies it before it ever runs. No warnings, no right-click workarounds.

Your data stays local

Nothing is uploaded. Reports are generated on your Mac and only leave it if you choose to share them.

FAQ

How do I install an app?

Download the .dmg, open it, and drag the app into your Applications folder. Launch it from there.

How does licensing work?

After checkout you'll get an email with your license key and download link. Paste the key into the app's Activate window. You can use it on your own Macs.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes — every download works as a free trial. Buy a license when you're ready to unlock everything.

What's your refund policy?

If an app doesn't work for you, email us within 14 days for a full refund.

Why does the app ask for Full Disk Access?

Some readings (like drive SMART data) need extra permission. The app explains exactly what it needs and why, and works with reduced features if you decline.

I lost my license key.

Email support@anothernetworkingguy.com from the address you bought with and we'll resend it.
