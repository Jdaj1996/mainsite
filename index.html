Why not the App Store?

Real diagnostics need things sandboxed App Store apps aren't allowed: a privileged helper, system log streams, and network commands that need root. Distributing directly lets the apps do their job.

Still safe to install

Every build is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS verifies it before it ever runs. No warnings, no right-click workarounds.

Your data stays local

Nothing is uploaded. Logs and evidence bundles are written on the Mac and only leave it when someone exports them.

FAQ

When can I get MacDiag?

MacDiag is in development. See what it does, or email us to hear when it launches.

How do I install an app?

Each app ships as a signed, notarized installer package. Open it on a single Mac, or upload it to your MDM (Mosyle, Jamf Pro and others) to push it to a fleet.

How does licensing work?

After checkout you'll get an email with your license key and download link. Paste the key into the app's Activate window. You can use it on your own Macs.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes — every download works as a free trial. Buy a license when you're ready to unlock everything.

What's your refund policy?

If an app doesn't work for you, email us within 14 days for a full refund.

Why does an app ask for an administrator password?

Some readings and controls need root, so they run through a small helper that only accepts a fixed list of commands. Installing it, and using controls that change system settings, asks for an administrator. Read-only views work without it.

I lost my license key.

Email support@anothernetworkingguy.com from the address you bought with and we'll resend it.
